## 课文金句

```
We do not stop trying because we are old; we grow old because we stop trying.
我们不会因为变老而停止努力；我们因为停止努力才变老。

Anyone can grow older — that doesn't take any talent or ability. The idea is to grow up by always finding the opportunity in change.
谁都会变老 — 那不需要任何天赋。关键是，要不停地在变化中寻找机遇，才能成长。
```
