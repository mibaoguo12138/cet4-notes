## 课文金句

```
Guochao first caught public attention overseas when a Chinese sportswear brand achieved huge success in showing products with traditional Chinese cultural elements at an international fashion week in 2018.

As a vehicle for Chinese culture, these domestic brands are bound to make a lasting difference in the world.
```
