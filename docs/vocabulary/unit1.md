## 课文重点语法

### as引导让步状语从句 (倒装)
```
Popular as they are, these half-moon-shaped dumplings are by no means quick to make.
尽管饺子很受欢迎，但制作起来绝不快捷。
```

### ground 四级核心考点
| 词性 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| n. 地面 | 1. 地面；土地 | The boy slipped and fell to the ground. |
| n. 理由 | 2. 根据；理由（多用复数 **grounds**）| on the grounds of = 以…为理由 |
| n. 共同点 | 3. **common ground** 共同点 | reach common ground |
| v. | **be grounded in/on** 基于 | Her argument is grounded in solid evidence. |
| v. | 使停飞 | All flights were grounded. |
| adj. | 切碎的；磨碎的 | ground meat / ground coffee |
