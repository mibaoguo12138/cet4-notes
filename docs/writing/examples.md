## 范文1：如何丰富校园生活

**Directions:** Suppose the Student Union is collecting opinions on improving its work. Write a response suggesting how it can better enrich student life.

---

It is beyond dispute that enriching campus life is essential to college students' all-round development. Therefore, to make more students embrace the vibrancy of campus life, I would like to put forward several practical and feasible suggestions.

First of all, increase support for campus interest clubs in order to attract more students to join them. In addition, practical activities such as visiting enterprises and holding job-hunting sharing sessions enable students to get in touch with society in advance, which can enhance students' practical and innovative abilities. In the end, we should not neglect cultural and sports activities either. We can organize more activities like art exhibitions and fun sports events, which are not only beneficial to physical and mental health but also offer university students opportunities to relax.

On the whole, it is quite essential to have more colorful activities to enrich our campus life. Only by putting these suggestions into practice will our campus life become more diverse and vibrant.

## 范文2：跨文化沟通能力

**Directions:** Your university is organizing a forum on developing students' cross-cultural communication abilities. Write an essay to express your view.

---

With the acceleration of globalization, an increasing number of universities have come to realize the necessity of developing students' cross-cultural communication abilities. Therefore, it is my belief that we should give priority to cultivating this essential skill.

Developing cross-cultural communication abilities can bring multiple benefits. For one thing, it can broaden the horizons of youngsters. When communicating with people from different cultural backgrounds, students are exposed to a rich variety of customs and viewpoints, which not only enriches their understanding of the world but also encourages them to appreciate the diversity that exists across different societies. For another, if students have excellent cross-cultural communication abilities, they will be better equipped to handle international cooperation tasks. On the contrary, if they fail to have such ability, they will miss a lot of opportunities in their future careers.

By and large, cross-cultural communication abilities play a crucial role in students' personal growth and career development. As college students, we should strive to learn foreign languages and actively participate in international activities to improve our communication abilities.

## 范文3：新闻报告 — 志愿活动

**Directions:** Write a news report on a volunteer activity organized by the Student Union to help elderly people in the neighborhood.

---

On June 6, Sunday, a volunteer activity in the local Nursing House was organized by the Student Union, which proved to be a success.

The activity was aimed at encouraging students to visit the elderly at the Nursing House and help elderly people deal with the troubles in their life. Many students volunteered to participate in this good deed and were engaged in helping the elderly out by making their meals, washing their clothes and chatting with them. When asked about those volunteers' feelings about such an experience, all of them responded with a smile, saying "What a wonderful experience! I really appreciate this activity because it makes me learn to care about those who are in need."

To sum up, the activity was quite successful not only for the elderly but also for those students involved.
