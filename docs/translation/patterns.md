# 🌏 翻译必背30个句型

| # | 句型结构 | 例句 |
|---|----------|------|
| 1 | **It is + adj. + that...** | It is conceivable that knowledge plays an important part in our life. |
| 2 | **It is + adj. + to do** | She had said what it was necessary to say. |
| 3 | **祈使句 + and/or + 陈述句** | Work hard, and you will finally be able to reach your goal. |
| 4 | **as + many/much + as** | Visitors spend only half as much money in Leeds as in London. |
| 5 | **倍数 + as + adj. + as** | The pool is three times as big as it was ten years ago. |
| 6 | **倍数 + more...than** | Smoking kills seven times more people each year than auto accidents. |
| 7 | **not as/so...as** | The pollution is not as serious as they suggested. |
| 8 | **no more...than** | She is no more fit to be a manager than a schoolgirl would be. |
| 9 | **Nothing is more...than** | Nothing is more important than to receive education. |
| 10 | **smell/taste of + n.** | They escaped into a cave that smelt of terror. |
