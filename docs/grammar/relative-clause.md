# 定语从句 + 分词作状语

## which 引导的非限制性定语从句

```
普通: Jinggangshan is between Hunan and Jiangxi. It is called "the red cradle of Chinese revolution."

升级: Jinggangshan is located at the boundary between Hunan Province and Jiangxi Province, which is recognized as "the red cradle of Chinese revolution" because of its glorious history.
```

## 分词作状语

```
普通: I am a student, and I like to read and do sports.

升级1: I am a student, liking to read and do sports. (现在分词)
升级2: I am a student, who likes to read and do sports. (定语从句)
```

## 翻译中的应用

```
如今，曾经令人生畏的冰雪景观吸引着来自四面八方的游客，这里已成为备受欢迎的旅游目的地。

Nowadays, the once formidable icy and snowy landscapes are attracting tourists from all directions, which have become a highly popular tourist destination.
```

## 分词作状语 + 定语从句组合

```
Through this technology, rice has stronger drought and disease resistance, making significant contributions to global food security.

The green mountains and clear waters have promoted tourism in the area, generating considerable economic benefits.
```
