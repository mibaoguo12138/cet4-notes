# 主谓一致 (Subject-Verb Agreement)

## 规则1：伴随结构不影响主谓一致

主语后接 `including`, `together with`, `as well as`, `in addition to`, `rather than`, `such as`, `more than`, `no less than` 等引导的词组时，**谓语动词形式依主语的单复数而定**。

```
The coach, as well as his players, IS excited.
The young lady, together with her two little dogs, TAKES a walk every evening.
Important skills, such as leadership, ARE required for this position.
```

## 规则2：非谓语动词作主语 → 第三人称单数

```
Playing sports IS a great way to keep fit and have fun at the same time.
To try and fail IS better than not to try at all.
Living on that island for three months WAS an unforgettable experience.
To translate this into English NEEDS careful work.
```

## 规则3：倒装句中的主谓一致

前置谓语动词的形式应与后面主语的单复数保持一致。

```
Only through hard work IS one able to achieve success.
Just down the street LIVES a famous local artist.
Parked along the road WERE six heavy trucks.
He is not the only football fan in his family, so ARE his brother, sister, and uncle.
```

## 规则4：不定代词作主语

`everyone`, `somebody`, `anyone` 等作主语时，谓语动词用单数，但相应代词可以复数：

```
Everyone can improve their listening skills with practice.
Anyone is welcome as long as they behave themselves.
```
